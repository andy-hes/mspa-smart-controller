# Commercial Licensing

## Summary

Commercial or professional use of this project requires a **separate written commercial license** from the author.

The public repository is intended for:

- personal use
- hobby use
- educational use
- non-commercial experimentation

## Commercial or professional use includes

The following are examples of use cases that require a commercial license:

- including the code, firmware, or design in a product that is sold
- shipping this as part of a commercial controller, kit, service, or appliance
- using it in customer installations for payment
- installer, integrator, consultant, workshop, or reseller use
- OEM or white-label use
- internal use by a company as part of paid operations or service delivery
- using the project as part of a managed platform, subscription, or support offering
- distributing modified versions as part of a commercial offer

If there is doubt about whether your use is commercial, treat it as commercial and request a license.

## What a commercial license can cover

A commercial license can define:

- permitted use case
- number of devices, products, or installations
- support expectations
- attribution requirements
- branding rules
- modification and redistribution rights
- pricing and payment terms
