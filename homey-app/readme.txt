MSpa Smart Restore is a Homey Pro app for controlling an MSpa hot tub that has been modified with an ESP32-based local controller.

The app talks to the ESP32 over local HTTP and gives you practical controls for heater, filter, bubbles, target temperature, and safe restore behavior after power loss.
